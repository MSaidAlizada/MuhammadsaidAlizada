Notes taken from Onur Mutlu lecture on how to do paper reviews: [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tOL6FANAJ8c)

### 1. Brief Summary
- What is the problem paper is trying to solve? 
- What are the key insights?
- What is the key contribution?
- What is the most important takeaways?

### 2. Strengths
- Order them from the strongest to weakest strengths
- Does the paper solve the problem well?

### 3. Weaknesses
- Again we should order them from biggest weakness to the smallest
- Should think critically on where there is room for improvement

### 4. Can you do better?
- Present thoughts/ideas

### 5. What have you learnt/enjoyed/disliked and why?

### Advice
- Be critical
- Always think of better ways to solve the problem
- Do background reading
